## Implementation constraints

1. Accept a requirement as met when its value has a source. If a field is self-reported, show its provenance. Unknown information returns Needs confirmation. A failed mandatory rule takes precedence.
2. Keep entry deadlines, entry conditions, assessed performance and optional preparation as separate concepts. A student who lacks a reviewed image or pitch can still meet the organizer's entry conditions.
3. Treat CHAMPUS skill names, examples, and indicator-to-opportunity links as editorial mappings. Preserve the publisher and source of the underlying criterion.
4. Store calendar-day precision separately from an exact UTC instant. Use organizer time where it is known. Never show a made-up clock time.
5. Publish the reviewed sources and retain each official destination. Keep paper requirements, team confirmations, and student evidence out of public catalog records.
