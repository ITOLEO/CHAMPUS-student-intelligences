## How one class supports different goals

**Decision.** Keep approved outcomes, assessment criteria and academic prerequisites under teacher or institutional control. Let a student change a personal goal, practice format, optional activity and sequence where prerequisites permit. Preserve the authority, source and version for each requirement. Mark all new mappings as proposed until an authorized teacher reviews them. Curriculum flexibility can apply to different dimensions, including content and pedagogy. The OECD account informs this distinction. Its school-system analysis does not evaluate this pilot. [OECD on curriculum flexibility](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/curriculum-flexibility-and-autonomy_eccbbac2-en/full-report/component-3.html)

**Worked case.** Two students take Digital Marketing. CHAMPUS proposes an outcome for the teacher to review. “Design a campaign asset for one audience and justify the call to action.” Both students must identify the audience, produce a suitable asset, and explain the link between the audience and the intended response.

| Person and goal | Assigned or chosen practice | Evidence available to the teacher |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Student who wants to build websites | Create a mobile landing page for an audience of choice. Add one call to action. | A working page and a short rationale identify the audience and intended response. |
| Student who wants to work in photography | Create a photo sequence for the same brief. Choose a sequence, caption and call to action. | Annotated images and a short rationale identify the audience and intended response. |
| Teacher | Assess both submissions against the shared campaign criteria. Request a correction when an artifact lacks evidence. | The result names the indicator, artifact version, reviewer and reason. |

Either student can choose either task. A task that assesses coding must require code or observable coding performance. A photo does not establish that result. Use aligned outcomes, learning tasks and evidence. A teacher can update the next task when the work shows a specific gap. Ask the student to record a goal, take action, inspect the result and choose a revision. The research supports these design principles. It supplies no effect size for CHAMPUS. [Biggs on constructive alignment](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00138871) · [Black and Wiliam on formative assessment](https://kclpure.kcl.ac.uk/portal/en/publications/developing-the-theory-of-formative-assessment/) · [Panadero on self-regulated learning](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2017.00422/full)

## When preferences can change a next action

Let a student choose a goal and an optional work preference. “I want to rehearse with a peer” can put peer rehearsal first. Show the specific reason and a control to change it. Store the student's statement separately from confidence, submitted work, observed performance and the teacher's review. Skipping or deleting a preference must leave the same academic requirements and eligible opportunities available.

The MBTI publisher describes ethical limits on using type to direct a person's choices. O*NET describes an interest inventory for career exploration. FotoKarakter describes its own service. The reviewed materials supply no independent evidence that a face, photo or color can establish a student's competence or match to a course. Keep these instruments distinct. Ask for an explicit preference. Keep portraits, color assignments and inferred personality types out of academic decisions. Show any voluntary reflection to the student and to other people only through an explicit, scoped choice. [MBTI code of ethics](https://www.myersbriggs.org/using-type-as-a-professional/mbti-code-of-ethics/) · [O*NET Interest Profiler](https://www.onetcenter.org/IP.html) · [FotoKarakter service description](https://fotokarakter.com/why-us)

A student's preferred format can make practice easier to start. Pashler and colleagues found inadequate evidence for assigning instruction on the basis of fixed learning styles. Their study addresses learning styles. It does not test the MBTI or FotoKarakter. Assess the artifact against the indicator, and allow the student to try another format. [Pashler and colleagues](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1539-6053.2009.01038.x)

## Role and evidence limits for tutoring

| Actor | Input and action | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Student tutor | Use the signed-in student's permitted course material, current goal and selected work. Ask what they have tried. Offer one hint or source and ask them to explain the result. | Keep another student's records out of retrieval. A hint, model output or completed chat cannot verify a competency. |
| Teacher assistant | Draft suggestions for an assignment or a feedback message against the selected course indicator. Cite the course document or label a CHAMPUS suggestion. | The authorized teacher reviews, edits and approves each consequential decision. The assistant cannot publish a course outcome or grade. |
| Industry partner | Describe a workplace task, expected evidence and opportunity conditions. Link to a dated organizer source. | A partner can propose a requirement. Teacher review, private learner work and the institutional curriculum have separate permissions. |
| Program coordinator | View the records their institution authorizes. Check source ownership and changes to curriculum mappings. | Access follows the membership and assigned scope. A role name in a prompt or user input grants no permission. |

Check access on the server before retrieval. Send only the information required for the selected task. Keep private reflections outside shared dashboards. If the source is missing, say what is unknown. If the external model is unavailable, provide stored guidance with an accurate label. For consequential or disputed advice, link the student to the authorized teacher. UNESCO addresses privacy and human agency. An experiment in high-school mathematics found that access to a general AI interface could impair later unaided performance and that tutoring safeguards mitigated this effect. This result supports testing independent performance in our own setting. [UNESCO guidance](https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/guidance-generative-ai-education-and-research) · [Bastani and colleagues](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2422633122)

## Review before a public claim

1. Ask the same two students to describe the shared outcome and how each practice fits their goal. Record misunderstandings.
2. Change or delete a preference. Verify that it changes only optional guidance. Recheck eligibility and the teacher's result.
3. Ask the tutor for another student's work and for a grade change. Check the access decision and the resulting database state.
4. Ask the teacher to identify the source and approval state of one mapping. A catalog title alone cannot establish an approved outcome.
5. Record the result, date and evidence for each test. Distinguish a source-verified fact, a local product decision and a pilot observation in public claims.
